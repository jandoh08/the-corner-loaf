Common Questions

Quick answers about ordering.

How do I place an order?

Choose Today's order or Pre-order, pick your collection time and item, set the quantity and add your name. Then press "Send order on WhatsApp". WhatsApp opens with your order already written, and it is placed when you press send there.

What is the difference between a today's order and a pre-order?

A today's order is for pick-up today and only lists what is baked today. You can place one while there are pick-up times left today. A pre-order is for a later day: you choose the day you will collect, then pick from what is baked that day.

How far in advance do I need to order?

We ask for at least one day's notice before your chosen collection day. For large orders or custom items, two to three days is better. This gives us time to plan our bake and make sure everything is ready for you.

Can I cancel or change my order?

Yes, but please let us know as early as possible. If we have already started baking your order, we may not be able to cancel it. Changes to quantity are usually fine if you give us a day's notice.

Can I ask for something custom or a large order?

You can ask. Two to three days' notice is better for these. Send us a message or write to us on WhatsApp with what you need, and we will tell you what we can do.

Is there a limit on how many I can order?

The order form takes one item at a time, up to 10 of that item per order. For several different items, send one order for each. For anything bigger, send us a message or write to us on WhatsApp, ideally two to three days ahead, and we will tell you what we can do.

How do I collect my order?

Come to the bakery at your agreed collection time. We will have your order bagged and labelled. If you are running late, let us know. We can usually hold orders for up to two hours after the agreed time.

Do you deliver?

We do not currently offer delivery. All orders must be collected from the bakery in person. We are looking at delivery options for the future.

About The Corner Loaf

Marcus started The Corner Loaf after years of baking at home for friends and family. What started as a hobby turned into a proper small bakery when demand grew too much to keep up with. Everything is made by hand in small batches, no mass production, no shortcuts.
